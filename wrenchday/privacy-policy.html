Wrenchday Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Wrenchday ("the App") is a vehicle maintenance log. The records you enter stay in the App's local storage. Purchase history is processed separately, as described below.

Data on Your Device

Vehicles, service records, and reminders you enter are stored on your device in the App's local storage.

  • The App has no accounts, no sign-up, and no server of ours that stores those records.
  • Deleting the App deletes this on-device data. A device backup (for example iCloud or a computer backup) may include it; that backup is handled by Apple, not by us.

Third-Party Services

The App uses RevenueCat to process in-app purchases and validate entitlements through the App Store. On the App Store privacy label this is declared as Purchases › Purchase History, used for App Functionality and Analytics. It is not linked to your identity and is not used for tracking.

Purchase history is sent to Apple and to RevenueCat so the App can unlock what you bought and so purchase activity can be measured. RevenueCat receives that purchase information together with an anonymous installation identifier generated by Apple's StoreKit framework — not your name, email, or maintenance records. We do not receive your payment card details. RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

Notifications

Reminder notifications are scheduled locally on your device.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and purchase history is not linked to identity.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new "Last updated" date.
