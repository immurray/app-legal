Wrenchday Support

Wrenchday is a vehicle maintenance log and reminder app for iPhone. Add your vehicles and service records, and Wrenchday can remind you before maintenance is due. Your records stay on your device — no account and no sign-up. Purchases are processed by Apple and RevenueCat, as described in the Privacy Policy.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does Wrenchday Pro include?

Wrenchday Pro is optional paid access sold through the App Store: auto-renewing monthly and yearly subscriptions, and a one-time lifetime purchase. Current pricing and any free-trial terms are shown in the app before you purchase.

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase, open Wrenchday, go to Settings, and tap Restore Purchases. Your Pro access will be restored. Restoring purchases does not transfer your vehicles or service records, because those records are stored only on the device.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone, open Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions, select Wrenchday, and tap Cancel Subscription. You can also manage subscriptions at apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to avoid renewal. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription. A one-time purchase is not a subscription and is not cancelled this way.

How do I request a refund?

Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple ID, and select the purchase.

I'm not getting reminders.

Check that notifications are allowed for Wrenchday in Settings → Notifications → Wrenchday, and that Focus / Do Not Disturb is not silencing them. Reminders are scheduled locally and need the device to be on.

Where is my data stored?

The vehicles and service records you enter are stored on your device. Deleting the app deletes that on-device data. Purchase history is sent to Apple and RevenueCat for App Functionality and Analytics; it is not linked to your identity and is not used for tracking. See the Privacy Policy.
