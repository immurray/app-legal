Wrenchday Terms of Service

Last updated: November 1, 2026

1. The Service

Wrenchday ("the App") is a vehicle maintenance log and reminder application. It stores the vehicles and service records you enter locally on your device and can notify you before a service is due. It does not perform repairs, book a shop, or connect to your vehicle.

2. Purchases & Billing

  • Wrenchday offers optional paid access ("Pro") sold through the App Store: auto-renewing monthly and yearly subscriptions, and a one-time lifetime purchase. Pricing and any free-trial terms are shown on the paywall before purchase and managed by Apple.
  • Payment is charged to your Apple ID account at confirmation of purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.
  • You can manage or cancel a subscription anytime in your App Store account settings: https://apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions

3. No Professional Advice

The App provides a personal log and reminders for information only. Nothing in the App is mechanical, safety, legal, or professional advice. Follow the maintenance schedule from your vehicle's manufacturer and a qualified technician.

4. Your Data

Records you create remain on your device. Purchase history is processed as described in our Privacy Policy. Deleting the App deletes your on-device records; we recommend keeping your own copies.
