Barolog 支援

Barolog（氣壓頭痛預報）是 iPhone 上的 App，用來查看氣壓變化，並讓你在這台 iPhone 上記下自己的不適。頭痛紀錄留在這台裝置上：沒有帳號，沒有廣告，沒有雲端同步。購買由 Apple 與 RevenueCat 處理，說明見隱私政策。

不是醫療器材

本 App 不是醫療器材，不提供診斷或治療。

頭痛突然劇烈，或伴隨發燒、頸部僵硬、意識或肢體異常時，應立即就醫。

急救電話：台灣 119、香港 999、韓國 119、泰國 1669。

天氣資料

天氣資料由 Apple Weather 提供。https://weatherkit.apple.com/legal-attribution.html

常見問題

Barolog Pro 包含什麼？

今天的氣壓變化和基本紀錄免費。Pro 以買斷或自動續訂的月訂、年訂提供，由 App Store 收款。價格以購買前顯示的為準。買斷只扣一次，不會自動續訂。

如何取消訂閱？

訂閱由 Apple 管理。在 iPhone 上打開設定 → [你的名字] → 訂閱項目，選取 Barolog，點取消訂閱。也可以在 apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions 管理訂閱。請在本期結束至少 24 小時前取消，否則會自動續訂。刪除 App 不會取消訂閱。買斷只扣一次，不會自動續訂，也不用這樣取消。

如何申請退款？

App Store 購買的退款由 Apple 處理。前往 reportaproblem.apple.com，以你的 Apple ID 登入，並選取該筆購買。

資料存在哪裡？

頭痛紀錄（強度、時間、備註、有沒有服藥和藥名）只存在這台 iPhone 的 App 支援目錄，並且排除 iCloud 備份。沒有帳號，沒有廣告，沒有雲端同步。我們沒有伺服器，不會把位置上傳到自己的後端。刪除 App 會一併刪除本機紀錄。購買紀錄透過 RevenueCat 與 App Store 處理：RevenueCat 只收到匿名識別碼和 Apple 的購買收據，用來確認 Pro 是否有效，不關聯你的姓名，也不做跨 App 追蹤。見隱私政策。

換機前如何保留紀錄？

換機前請自行匯出 CSV 或 PDF。刪除 App 會一併刪除本機紀錄。選中的城市只留在這台手機。

可以不開定位嗎？

可以。只有在你同意「使用期間」定位時，才會把座標交給 Apple 天氣以取得氣壓。你也可以不開定位，改為自己選城市。定位和通知都可以在系統設定裡關閉。

Barolog Support

Barolog is an iPhone app for viewing changes in air pressure and recording your own discomfort on this iPhone. Headache records stay on this device: no account, no advertising, and no cloud sync. Purchases are handled by Apple and RevenueCat, as described in the Privacy Policy.

Not a Medical Device

This App is not a medical device and does not provide diagnosis or treatment.

If a headache is suddenly severe, or if it comes with fever, neck stiffness, or an abnormality of consciousness or of the limbs, get medical care immediately.

Emergency numbers: Taiwan 119, Hong Kong 999, South Korea 119, Thailand 1669.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does Barolog Pro include?

Today's air-pressure changes and basic records are free. Pro is offered as a one-time purchase or as an auto-renewing monthly or yearly subscription, and payment is collected by the App Store. The price is the price shown before purchase. A one-time purchase is charged only once and does not renew automatically.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone, open Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions, select Barolog, and tap Cancel Subscription. You can also manage subscriptions at apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period; otherwise it renews automatically. Deleting the App does not cancel the subscription. A one-time purchase is charged only once, does not renew automatically, and is not cancelled this way.

How do I request a refund?

Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple ID, and select the purchase.

Where is my data stored?

Headache records (intensity, time, notes, whether you took medication, and the medication name) exist only in this iPhone's app support directory and are excluded from iCloud backup. There is no account, no advertising, and no cloud sync. We have no server and do not upload location to a backend of our own. Deleting the App also deletes the on-device records. Purchase history is handled through RevenueCat and the App Store: RevenueCat receives only an anonymous identifier and Apple's purchase receipt, used to confirm whether Pro is valid. It is not associated with your name and is not used for cross-app tracking. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I keep my records when I change phones?

Export a CSV or PDF yourself before changing phones. Deleting the App also deletes the on-device records. The selected city stays only on this phone.

Can I leave location off?

Yes. Only when you allow location “While Using the App” are coordinates passed to Apple Weather to obtain air pressure. You can also leave location off and choose a city yourself. Location and notifications can both be turned off in system settings.
