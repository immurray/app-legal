Barolog 隱私政策

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Barolog（下稱「本 App」）用來查看氣壓變化，並讓你在這台 iPhone 上記下自己的不適。它不是醫療器材。

我們收集什麼

  • 購買紀錄：內購透過 RevenueCat 與 App Store 完成。RevenueCat 只收到匿名識別碼和 Apple 的購買收據，用來確認 Pro 是否有效。不關聯你的姓名，也不做跨 App 追蹤。RevenueCat 的說明：https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy
  • 位置：只有在你同意「使用期間」定位時，才會把座標交給 Apple 天氣以取得氣壓。你也可以不開定位，改為自己選城市。我們沒有伺服器，不會把位置上傳到自己的後端。選中的城市只留在這台手機。
  • 頭痛紀錄：強度、時間、備註、有沒有服藥和藥名，只存在這台 iPhone 的 App 支援目錄，並且排除 iCloud 備份。沒有帳號，沒有廣告，沒有雲端同步。

你的選擇

刪除 App 會一併刪除本機紀錄。換機前請自行匯出 CSV 或 PDF。定位和通知都可以在系統設定裡關閉。

兒童

本 App 不面向兒童類別，也不明知收集兒童的個人資料。

Barolog Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Barolog ("the App") is used to view changes in air pressure and to record your own discomfort on this iPhone. It is not a medical device.

What We Collect

  • Purchase history: In-app purchases are completed through RevenueCat and the App Store. RevenueCat receives only an anonymous identifier and Apple's purchase receipt, used to confirm whether Pro is valid. This is not associated with your name, and it is not used for cross-app tracking. RevenueCat's notice: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy
  • Location: Only when you allow location “While Using the App” are coordinates passed to Apple Weather to obtain air pressure. You can also leave location off and choose a city yourself. We have no server, and we do not upload location to a backend of our own. The selected city stays only on this phone.
  • Headache records: Intensity, time, notes, whether you took medication, and the medication name exist only in this iPhone's app support directory, and are excluded from iCloud backup. There is no account, no advertising, and no cloud sync.

Your Choices

Deleting the App also deletes the on-device records. Export a CSV or PDF yourself before changing phones. Location and notifications can both be turned off in system settings.

Children

The App is not directed at the children's category, and it does not knowingly collect children's personal data.
