Barolog Terms of Service

Last updated: November 2, 2026

1. The Service

Barolog shows alerts about changes in air pressure and saves the records you enter yourself. It is not a medical device, and it does not provide diagnosis, treatment, prevention, or dosing advice. The air-pressure curve cannot be used to judge whether you will have a headache. If you feel unwell, consult a qualified medical professional. If a dangerous situation listed in the specifications occurs, contact local emergency services immediately.
