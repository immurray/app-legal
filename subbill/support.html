SubBill Support

SubBill is a bill and subscription reminder app for iPhone. Add your recurring bills and subscriptions, and SubBill reminds you before each renewal date so you are never surprised by a charge. All your data stays on your device — no account, no sign-up, and no connection to your bank.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does SubBill Pro include?

The free tier lets you track up to 3 bills with full reminder functionality. SubBill Pro is an optional auto-renewing subscription sold through the App Store; current pricing and any free-trial terms are shown in the app before you purchase.

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase, open SubBill, go to Settings, and tap Restore Purchases. Your Pro access will be restored. Restoring purchases does not transfer your bill list, because bills are stored only on the device.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone, open Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions, select SubBill, and tap Cancel Subscription. You can also manage subscriptions at apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to avoid renewal. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription.

How do I request a refund?

Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple ID, and select the purchase.

I'm not getting reminders.

Check that notifications are allowed for SubBill in Settings → Notifications → SubBill, and that Focus / Do Not Disturb is not silencing them. Reminders are scheduled locally and need the device to be on.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your device. Deleting the app deletes your data. See the Privacy Policy.
