SubBill Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

SubBill ("the App") is built to be private by design.

Data We Collect

Your bill data stays on your device. We do not run any servers that receive your bills, and we do not collect your name, email, contacts, location, or financial account details.

  • All bills, subscription records, prices, and reminders you enter are stored only on your device in the App's local storage. They are never sent to us or to any third party.
  • The App has no accounts, no sign-up, and no server-side storage of your bills.
  • The App does not connect to your bank, credit card, or any financial account, and never asks for financial credentials.

Purchase History

If you buy or restore a SubBill Pro subscription, your purchase history (App Store transaction and subscription status data, associated with an anonymous app user ID) is processed by Apple and by our subscription provider RevenueCat so the App can unlock and verify SubBill Pro.

  • Used only for app functionality (providing and restoring your subscription).
  • Not linked to your identity: no name, email, or bill data is attached to it.
  • Not used for tracking and not shared with data brokers or advertisers. The App contains no advertising or analytics SDKs.

Third-Party Services

Apple (App Store / StoreKit) processes payments for subscriptions. Apple's privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

RevenueCat processes subscription purchases and validates entitlements through the App Store, as described above. RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

Notifications

Reminder notifications are scheduled locally on your device. We cannot see them.

Children

The App does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new "Last updated" date.
