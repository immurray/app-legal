SubBill Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

SubBill ("the App") is built to be private by design.

Data We Collect

None. SubBill does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal data.

  • All bills, subscription records, prices, and reminders you enter are stored only on your device in the App's local storage.
  • The App has no accounts, no sign-up, and no server-side storage.
  • The App does not connect to your bank, credit card, or any financial account, and never asks for financial credentials.

Third-Party Services

The App uses RevenueCat solely to process subscription purchases and validate entitlements through the App Store. RevenueCat receives only an anonymous installation identifier generated by Apple's StoreKit framework — no name, email, or bill data. RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

Notifications

Reminder notifications are scheduled locally on your device. We cannot see them.

Children

The App does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new "Last updated" date.
