SubBill Terms of Service

Last updated: September 25, 2026

1. The Service

SubBill ("the App") is a bill and subscription reminder application. It stores your bills and subscriptions locally on your device and notifies you before renewal dates. It does not pay bills, manage funds, or connect to financial institutions.

2. Subscriptions & Billing

  • SubBill offers an auto-renewing subscription ("Pro") sold through the App Store. Pricing, tiers, and free-trial terms are shown on the paywall before purchase and managed by Apple.
  • Payment is charged to your Apple ID account at confirmation of purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.
  • You can manage or cancel your subscription anytime in your App Store account settings: https://apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions
  • The free tier lets you track up to 3 bills with full reminder functionality.

3. No Financial Advice

The App provides reminders and price-change history for information only. Nothing in the App is financial, legal, or professional advice.

4. Your Data

All content you create remains on your device. See our Privacy Policy. Deleting the App deletes your data; we recommend keeping your own records.
