Fevernote 隱私政策

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Fevernote（下稱「本 App」）是給家長在這台 iPhone 上為孩子記下體溫、用藥與症狀的工具。它不是醫療器材，不算劑量，也不判斷要不要就醫。

我們收集什麼

  • 購買紀錄：內購透過 RevenueCat 與 App Store 完成。RevenueCat 只收到匿名識別碼和 Apple 的購買收據，用來確認 Pro 是否有效。不關聯你的姓名，也不做跨 App 追蹤。RevenueCat 的說明：https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy
  • 健康與孩子紀錄：孩子的暱稱、出生年月（只用來顯示年齡）、顏色、體溫、家長自己填的藥名與劑量文字、症狀與備註，只存在這台 iPhone 的 App 支援目錄，並且排除 iCloud 備份。沒有帳號，沒有廣告，沒有分析 SDK，沒有雲端同步，我們也沒有自有伺服器。

你的選擇

在設定裡可以「刪除全部資料」，或在孩子詳情裡刪除單一孩子。刪除 App 會一併刪除本機紀錄。通知可以在系統設定裡關閉。

兒童

本 App 面向家長，不進兒童類別，也不明知收集兒童的個人資料。紀錄由家長輸入並留在家長的裝置上。

Fevernote Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Fevernote ("the App") is a tool for parents to record a child's temperature, medication, and symptoms on this iPhone. It is not a medical device, does not calculate doses, and does not judge whether to seek care.

What We Collect

  • Purchase history: In-app purchases are completed through RevenueCat and the App Store. RevenueCat receives only an anonymous identifier and Apple's purchase receipt, used to confirm whether Pro is valid. This is not associated with your name, and it is not used for cross-app tracking. RevenueCat's notice: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy
  • Health and child records: The child's nickname, birth year/month (only used to show age), color, temperatures, medicine names and dose text the parent types, symptoms, and notes exist only in this iPhone's app support directory and are excluded from iCloud backup. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics SDK, and no cloud sync. We have no server of our own.

Your Choices

In Settings you can "Delete all data", or delete one child from that child's detail screen. Deleting the App also deletes the on-device records. Notifications can be turned off in system settings.

Children

The App is for parents, is not in the children's category, and does not knowingly collect children's personal data. Records are entered by a parent and stay on the parent's device.
