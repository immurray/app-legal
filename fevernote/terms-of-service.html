Fevernote Terms of Service

Last updated: November 5, 2026

1. The Service

Fevernote lets a parent record a child's temperature, self-entered medication, and symptoms on this iPhone, and export them for a clinician. It is not a medical device, and it does not provide diagnosis, treatment, prevention, or dosing advice, and it does not judge whether care is needed. If you feel unwell, consult a qualified medical professional. In an emergency, contact local emergency services immediately.

2. Purchases

  • Free: one child, all logging features, and the last 7 days of history. Pro (more than one child, full history, PDF export, custom reminders) is offered as a one-time purchase or as an auto-renewing monthly or yearly subscription, and payment is collected by the App Store. The price is the price shown before purchase.
