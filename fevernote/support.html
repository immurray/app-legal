Fevernote 支援

Fevernote 是 iPhone 上給家長用的發燒與用藥紀錄 App。孩子與健康紀錄留在這台裝置上：沒有帳號，沒有廣告，沒有雲端同步。購買由 Apple 與 RevenueCat 處理，說明見隱私政策。

不是醫療器材

本 App 不是醫療器材，不提供診斷或治療，不算劑量，也不判斷是否該就醫。藥名與劑量文字由家長自己輸入，App 只原樣記錄。

孩子出現急症、意識異常或你擔心時，請立刻聯絡當地急救或合格醫療人員。

急救／諮詢電話（外鏈，僅供參考）：日本 #8000、台灣 119、香港 999、韓國 119、泰國 1669。

常見問題

Fevernote Pro 包含什麼？

免費：1 個孩子、全部記錄功能、最近 7 天歷史。Pro 另含多個孩子、全部歷史、PDF 匯出、自訂提醒，以買斷或自動續訂的月訂、年訂提供，由 App Store 收款。價格以購買前顯示的為準。買斷只扣一次，不會自動續訂。

如何取消訂閱？

訂閱由 Apple 管理。在 iPhone 上打開設定 → [你的名字] → 訂閱項目，選取 Fevernote，點取消訂閱。也可以在 apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions 管理訂閱。請在本期結束至少 24 小時前取消，否則會自動續訂。刪除 App 不會取消訂閱。買斷只扣一次，不會自動續訂，也不用這樣取消。

如何申請退款？

App Store 購買的退款由 Apple 處理。前往 reportaproblem.apple.com，以你的 Apple ID 登入，並選取該筆購買。

資料存在哪裡？如何刪除？

孩子與健康紀錄只存在這台 iPhone 的 App 支援目錄，並且排除 iCloud 備份。沒有帳號，沒有廣告，沒有雲端同步。我們沒有自有伺服器。設定 →「刪除全部資料」可清空；孩子詳情可刪除單一孩子；刪除 App 也會一併刪除本機紀錄。購買紀錄透過 RevenueCat 與 App Store 處理：RevenueCat 只收到匿名識別碼和 Apple 的購買收據，用來確認 Pro 是否有效。見隱私政策。

如何恢復購買？

在付費牆或設定 → Fevernote Pro →「恢復購買」。

Fevernote Support

Fevernote is an iPhone app for parents to log fever and medication. Child and health records stay on this device: no account, no advertising, and no cloud sync. Purchases are handled by Apple and RevenueCat, as described in the Privacy Policy.

Not a Medical Device

This App is not a medical device and does not provide diagnosis or treatment, does not calculate doses, and does not judge whether care is needed. Medicine names and dose text are typed by the parent; the App only stores them as entered.

If a child has an emergency, an abnormality of consciousness, or you are worried, contact local emergency services or a qualified medical professional immediately.

Emergency / advice numbers (outbound links only): Japan #8000, Taiwan 119, Hong Kong 999, South Korea 119, Thailand 1669.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does Fevernote Pro include?

Free: one child, all logging features, and the last 7 days of history. Pro also includes more than one child, full history, PDF export, and custom reminders, offered as a one-time purchase or as an auto-renewing monthly or yearly subscription, with payment collected by the App Store. The price is the price shown before purchase. A one-time purchase is charged only once and does not renew automatically.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone, open Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions, select Fevernote, and tap Cancel Subscription. You can also manage subscriptions at apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period; otherwise it renews automatically. Deleting the App does not cancel the subscription. A one-time purchase is charged only once, does not renew automatically, and is not cancelled this way.

How do I request a refund?

Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple ID, and select the purchase.

Where is my data stored, and how do I delete it?

Child and health records exist only in this iPhone's app support directory and are excluded from iCloud backup. There is no account, no advertising, and no cloud sync. We have no server of our own. Settings → "Delete all data" clears everything; a child's detail screen can delete one child; deleting the App also deletes the on-device records. Purchase history is handled through RevenueCat and the App Store: RevenueCat receives only an anonymous identifier and Apple's purchase receipt, used to confirm whether Pro is valid. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I restore purchases?

On the paywall, or in Settings → Fevernote Pro → "Restore Purchases".
